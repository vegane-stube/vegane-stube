Privacy for submissions

When you submit a recipe by email, we use your email address, recipe text, photo, and, if supplied, your name to handle the submission and answer questions.

Your email address is not published. If you do not provide a name, the contribution is published anonymously. We delete the data when we no longer need it.

Contributions, text, and photos

By submitting, you confirm that you have the necessary rights to the recipe text and photo. You allow Vegane Stube to edit, translate, and publish them worldwide and free of charge for the project. You retain your underlying rights.

Submissions may be edited or not published. No payment is provided. Your name is published only if you enter one in the template.

Recipes, allergens, and food safety

Recipes are editorially reviewed, but published without guarantee. Check ingredients, quantities, preparation, and allergens yourself before use. Vegan products may still contain traces depending on the manufacturer.

Vegane Stube is not responsible for harm caused by preparing or using a recipe. If you have allergies, intolerances, or doubts, seek appropriate professional advice.
