A community project

Vegan recipes from and for South Tyrol.

Are you looking for vegan ideas from South Tyrol, or would you like to rediscover familiar dishes? This is a growing collection of recipes from the region. We do not take “traditional” too seriously: family variations, modern interpretations, and original ideas are welcome.

Vegane Stube is an ongoing community project and only works with everyone’s help. If you notice something strange or something that does not work, please tell us.
